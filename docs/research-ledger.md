## Synthesis

1. **The educational opening is real:** poorly designed answer delivery can bypass productive reasoning; formative feedback, metacognition, retrieval, and explanation are stronger design anchors.
2. **The obvious product category is crowded:** camera solving, handwritten diagnosis, and first-error detection already exist.
3. **The differentiation is the complete loop:** evidence-linked hypothesis → personalized misconception twin → falsifying counterexample → learner teach-back → fresh transfer.
4. **The central technical risk is not OCR alone:** it is plausible but incorrect interpretation. AhaWin therefore adds strict structured output, semantic checks, confidence, a transcription checkpoint, and an honest failure path.
5. **Impact remains a hypothesis:** automated and synthetic tests establish product quality, not improved learning outcomes.
