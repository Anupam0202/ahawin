    WHY ROLE REVERSAL WORKS

    The AI becomes the novice.

    01

    Evidence, not mind-reading

    AhaWin anchors a possible misconception to the exact step that supports it and keeps uncertainty visible.

    02

    A rule you can break

    A tailored counterexample turns an abstract correction into a contradiction the learner can inspect.

    03

    Explanation creates effort

    Teach-back requires the learner to reconstruct the principle instead of copying a polished solution.

    04

    Transfer is the finish line

    A fresh item checks whether the repaired rule survives beyond the original problem.
